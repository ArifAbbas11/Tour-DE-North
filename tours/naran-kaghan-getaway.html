Naran Kaghan

Naran Kaghan Getaway

Lake Saif-ul-Malook, Babusar Top and the cool green valley of Kaghan.

Duration3 Days / 2 Nights
Group size2 - 12 people
Best timeApril - October
DepartureLahore, Karachi, Islamabad

Overview

A short and refreshing break in the Kaghan Valley. Ideal for families and first-time visitors who want mountains, rivers and lakes in just three days.

Itinerary

  1. Day 1: Islamabad to Naran

    Scenic drive via Balakot, evening at the river bank.

  2. Day 2: Lake Saif-ul-Malook

    Jeep ride to the lake, optional pony ride, Lalazar visit.

  3. Day 3: Babusar Top and return

    Drive up to Babusar Top and back to Islamabad.

Package includes

  • Hotel stay (twin sharing)
  • Breakfast and dinner
  • Transport as per itinerary
  • Jeep to Saif-ul-Malook

Package excludes

  • Lunch
  • Personal expenses
  • Pony rides and entrance tickets
Starting fromRs. 24,000 per person
