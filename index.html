FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the best time to visit Northern Pakistan?

April to October is the best time for Hunza, Skardu, Naran and most valleys. If you love snow, December to February is great for Murree, Nathia Gali and Swat.

How many days do I need?

For Naran or Swat, 3 to 4 days is enough. Hunza needs 6 to 7 days, and a Hunza + Skardu trip works best with 10 to 12 days.

What is included in the package price?

Each tour page lists exactly what is included and excluded. Usually it covers hotels, transport, a driver or guide and selected meals.

Can I customise my trip?

Yes. Use the Make My Trip form or message us on WhatsApp and we will adjust the route, hotels and budget for you.

How do I book?

Send a booking request from any tour page. Our team will contact you, confirm availability and share payment details.
